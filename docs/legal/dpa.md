## 1. Roles (register item 14)

For **call metadata generated by your organisation's use of the service** (calls your
business number forwards or bridges), your organisation is the **controller** and
Telocc is the **processor**. For **Telocc's own account, security, and (once it exists)
billing data**, Telocc is an **independent controller** in its own right (see
`docs/legal/privacy-notice.md`).

## 2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing

- **Subject matter:** provision of the Telocc call-forwarding/outbound-bridge service.
- **Duration:** for the term of your Telocc account, plus the retention/carve-out
  periods in `docs/legal/privacy-notice.md` §3 and §6.
- **Nature and purpose:** routing and logging calls to/from your business number
  (timestamp, direction, duration, status, calling/called number) exactly as configured
  by you (office hours, verified personal number, outbound bridge).
- **Categories of data subjects:** the callers who call, or are called by, your
  organisation's Telocc number.
- **Categories of personal data:** phone numbers, call timestamps/duration/status —
  no call recordings or content, no names beyond what a phone number itself might
  reveal.

## 4. Telocc's obligations as processor

Telocc will:

- process personal data only on your documented instructions (operating the service as
  you configure it), including regarding international transfers, unless required
  otherwise by EU or Member State law;
- ensure persons authorised to process the data are subject to confidentiality;
- implement the technical and organisational measures described in
  `docs/compliance/toms.md` (Art 32);
- respect the conditions for engaging sub-processors in §3 above;
- assist you, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to
  data-subject rights requests from your callers (register item 17) — in practice, by
  providing the export/erasure tooling described in `docs/legal/privacy-notice.md` §6
  and by direct support for anything that tooling doesn't cover;
- assist you with your own Art 32–36 obligations, including breach notification (§5
  below) and, where relevant, data protection impact assessments;
- at your choice, delete or return all personal data at the end of the service, subject
  to the retention carve-outs in `docs/legal/privacy-notice.md` §3/§6;
- make available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this
  addendum and allow for audits, subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality
  (**exact audit mechanics** `[OWNER/COUNSEL TO CONFIRM — proportionate to a
  single-owner-scale operation]`).

## 5. Breach notification (register item 21)

Telocc will notify you **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a personal data
breach affecting your organisation's call data, following the process in
`docs/runbook.md` §"Incident response". This assistance duty runs alongside — not
instead of — your own obligation as controller to assess and, if required, notify the
Czech Data Protection Authority (ÚOOÚ) and affected individuals.

## 6. Deletion on termination (register item 14)

On termination of your account, Telocc deletes your organisation's account data,
verified numbers, call log, KYC/regulatory documents, and sessions per
`docs/legal/privacy-notice.md` §6, and makes a best-effort request to sub-processors to
delete their own copies (e.g. Twilio call-record deletion via API). Carve-outs (e.g.
future billing/tax records) are stated there and updated when billing exists (OWN-9).

## 7. International transfers (register item 16)

See §3 above and `docs/compliance/transfer-register.md`. Each sub-processor's transfer
mechanism (EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses) is stated
there and kept current, including tracking the pending CJEU appeal against the DPF
(case C-703/25 P).
